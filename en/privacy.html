1. Who is responsible for your data

Revum Group LLC, which offers career coaching services under the Pitcheamos brand, with its address at 3801 Avalon Park E Blvd, FL 2 STE 217, Orlando, FL 32828, USA, is responsible for processing your personal data under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

Privacy contact: hola@pitcheamos.com

2. What data we collect

Only what you give us through the forms on pitcheamos.com and business.pitcheamos.com, or when booking a call:

  • Name, email and, if you choose to, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • What you tell us in your message about your job search or career.
  • If you contact us as a company through business.pitcheamos.com: company name, team size and the service you are interested in.
  • Browsing data on this site (see the cookies section).
  • If you fill in the diagnostic form: your LinkedIn profile, the CV you attach (stored privately in HubSpot and seen only by Pitcheamos), your job-search situation, the roles and industries you are targeting, and anything you tell us. We delete the CV if you ask us to, or once the coaching ends and we no longer need it.
  • If you use the simulator and then contact us: the answers you picked (hours per week, market, level, use of contacts and tailored CV) and the estimated result.

We don't ask for sensitive personal data (health, religion, ethnic origin, etc.). Please don't include it in your messages.

The simulator runs in your browser: if you don't submit the contact form, nothing you enter there is stored.

3. What we use it for

Necessary purposes (we can't help you without them):

  • Replying to your message and setting up a call or diagnostic session.
  • Booking, confirming or rescheduling appointments.
  • If you hire a service, delivering it and following up.
  • Measuring and improving how the site works.

Additional purpose (you can opt out anytime):

  • Occasionally sending you Pitcheamos content or news.

To opt out, email hola@pitcheamos.com with the subject "No news". This doesn't affect how we work with you.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

We use providers that store information on our behalf and only for these purposes: GitHub (website hosting), HubSpot (form handling, contact management and meeting scheduling), Cloudflare (processing of company forms), Amplitude (website usage analytics) and Google Workspace (email and calendar). Their servers may be outside Mexico.

We would only hand over data to an authority if a law or court order requires it.

5. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. You can also withdraw your consent or ask us to limit its use.

Email hola@pitcheamos.com with your name, the email you contacted us from, and what you'd like us to do. We reply within 20 business days and, where applicable, act on it within the following 15 business days.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as we're in contact or working together, and then as long as legally required. If we don't end up working together, we delete your data 12 months after your last contact, or sooner if you ask.

7. Cookies

We use Amplitude to understand how the site is used and improve it. Amplitude uses cookies and browser storage to record technical and browsing data: pages you visit, clicks, device type, browser and approximate location. It also records how you move through the page (session replay), including what you type into forms. Only we can see these recordings. If you leave us your details, we link that browsing to your email to serve you better. Amplitude also lets us show you messages, short surveys or test different versions of the page.

We don't use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. We load our font from Google Fonts, which may log technical data from your browser (such as your IP address).

8. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll post the new version on this page with its update date.

By sending us your data through this website's forms, you agree to its processing as described in this privacy notice.

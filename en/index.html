How we work

A four-stage method. We start wherever you are today.

1

Assessment

Where you are, what you want and what’s blocking you. You leave with a concrete plan.

2

Positioning

Résumé, LinkedIn and your pitch aligned to one clear focus.

3

Interviews and case studies

Company research, mock interviews and feedback on your assignments.

4

Offer and negotiation

Range, package and how to ask, so you close on the best terms.

Confidence runs through the whole process: an achievements bank, prep before every interview and a debrief after. It’s not therapy: it’s method and practice.

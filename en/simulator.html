The Pitcheamos Method

Three levers that move your numbers

1

Focus and tailored applications

Fewer applications, better chosen and tailored to each posting. A tailored CV gets roughly twice as many interviews as a generic one.

2

Your network, with a method

Asking for referrals in a structured way. In Mexico, more than half of people get hired through contacts.

3

Interviews that close

Your pitch, mock interviews, questions that set you apart and follow-up after every interview, to turn more interviews into offers.

How we calculate

It’s an estimate, not a promise. We use public market data plus a few assumptions of our own, flagged as such. Your case may differ depending on your industry, your country and the market.

The rates we use
ActionTimeInterview chance
Application with a generic CV15 min2.5%
Application with a CV tailored to the posting45 min5.75%
Asking a contact for a referral30 min8% (1 in 5 contacts refers you × 40% of referrals reach an interview)
  • We estimate 15% of first interviews end in an offer, and 22% when you prepare your interviews with a method.
  • We assume at most 15 postings per week that truly fit your profile and 8 contacts per week you can ask for a referral. More hours don’t create new postings or contacts, and above 15 hours a week time counts half, because of fatigue.
  • We add the length of a hiring process to your search time: 3 weeks for junior, 4 for mid-level and 6 for senior.
  • If you’re looking abroad, we lower the rates by 40%: you compete with more candidates and many processes require work authorization.
  • The range goes from a pessimistic scenario (25% fewer interviews and offers) to an optimistic one (30% more).
Sources
  • Application → interview, between 3% and 4.7%: Ashby and ZipRecruiter data, 2026 and an analysis of 10M+ applications.
  • Tailored vs. generic CV, 5.75% and 2.68%: Easy-CV, 2026.
  • Referrals, 40% reach an interview: Ashby and Gem data, 2026. Referrals are 2.2× more likely to get an interview: Federal Reserve study.
  • Interview → offer, between 10% and 40% depending on the source: Appcast, Employ and Ashby benchmarks.
  • Hiring process length: 2026 time-to-hire benchmarks.
  • Job search in LATAM: INEGI, ENOE and INDEC, EPH. Hiring through contacts in Mexico, 54.5%: ENOE via Yahoo Finanzas.
  • Job search support programs, 2.67× more likely to find a job: meta-analysis of 47 programs.

Almost all conversion data comes from the US. For Latin America there’s good data on unemployment length and hiring channels, but not on interview rates.
